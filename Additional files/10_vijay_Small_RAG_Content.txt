BIOGRAPHY
Chandrasekaran Joseph Vijay (born 22 June 1974) is an Indian politician and former actor serving as the ninth chief minister of Tamil Nadu since May 2026. He is founder and president of Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK). Before politics, he was a leading Tamil cinema actor and among the highest-paid actors in India, receiving accolades including multiple Tamil Nadu State Film Awards and South Indian International Movie Awards.

Vijay was a child actor in the 1980s and became a leading man with Naalaiya Theerpu (1992), directed by his father S. A. Chandrasekhar. Romance films including Poove Unakkaga (1996), Love Today (1997), Kadhalukku Mariyadhai (1997), Thullatha Manamum Thullum (1999), Kushi (2000), Priyamaanavale (2000), and Friends (2001) established his popularity. He transitioned into an action star with Thirumalai (2003), Ghilli (2004), Madhurey (2004), Thirupaachi (2005), and Pokkiri (2007).

Major commercial successes from the 2010s onward include Thuppakki (2012), Kaththi (2014), Theri (2016), Mersal (2017), Sarkar (2018), Bigil (2019), Master (2021), Leo (2023), and The Greatest of All Time (2024).

Vijay consolidated his fan following through the social welfare organisation Vijay Makkal Iyakkam (VMI) in 2009 and engaged in philanthropic activities across Tamil Nadu. Later films such as Kaththi and Sarkar portrayed him as an angry protagonist fighting corruption, injustice, and social inequality, often bringing him into conflict with the state's ruling political establishment.[1]

In February 2024, Vijay announced his retirement from cinema and formal entry into politics. He launched TVK as a secular and egalitarian alternative to the established Dravidian parties. TVK secured 108 seats in the 2026 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly election, becoming the single-largest party and breaking the half-century-long bureaucratic duopoly of the DMK and the AIADMK.[2][3] With support from several smaller allied parties, Vijay was sworn in as chief minister on 10 May 2026.

EARLY LIFE
Chandrasekaran Joseph Vijay was born on 22 June 1974 in Madras.[4][5] His father, Chandrasekaran, is a film director; his mother, Shoba, is a playback singer and vocalist.[6] His father is a lapsed Catholic and his mother is an adherent of Hinduism.[7][8] Vijay had a sister, Vidhya, who died when she was two years old.[9]

Vijay attended Fathima school, Kodambakkam, and later Balalok school, Virugambakkam.[10][11] He pursued a bachelor's degree in visual communication from Loyola College but dropped out early to focus on acting.[12][13][14]

FILM CAREER — 1984–2002: DEBUT AND TRANSITION TO LEAD ROLES
- Vijay debuted as a child actor in Vetri, produced by P. S. Veerappa, in 1984.[15]
- He then appeared in Kudumbam (1984), Naan Sigappu Manithan (1985), Vasantha Raagam (1986), Sattam Oru Vilayaattu (1987), and Ithu Engal Neethi (1988), all directed by his father.
- Naalaiya Theerpu (1992) was his first lead role at age 18.[16] It failed at the box office and drew criticism for his looks and performance.
- Senthoorapandi (1993), alongside established hero Vijayakanth, was a hit and increased Vijay's popularity.[17]
- In Rasigan (1994), directed by his father, Vijay became popular as “Ilaya Thalapathy” (Young Commander).[18] He also debuted as a singer.[19]
- Deva (1995) was a box office hit with a 100-day cinema-hall run.[20]
- Rajavin Parvaiyile featured Vijay with actor Ajith Kumar,[21] followed by Vishnu and Chandralekha.[22][23]
- Coimbatore Mappillai (1996) was a box office hit.[24] Poove Unakkaga, directed by Vikraman, gave Vijay his initial break and popularity.[25]
- 1996 films also included Vasantha Vaasal, Maanbumigu Maanavan, and Selvaa.
- 1997 films included Kaalamellam Kaathiruppen, Love Today, Once More, Nerrukku Ner, and Fazil's Kadhalukku Mariyadhai. Once More featured veteran actor Sivaji Ganesan; Nerrukku Ner featured actor Suriya's marked debut.[26][27][28]
- Kadhalukku Mariyadhai attracted the family audience and earned Vijay his first Tamil Nadu State Film Award for Best Actor.[29][30] He was awarded Kalaimaamani by the Government of Tamil Nadu.[31]
- 1998 films: Ninaithen Vandhai, Priyamudan, Nilaave Vaa.
- Thullatha Manamum Thullum (1999) was a blockbuster and helped Vijay create a fan base in Kerala.[32][33] He won the MGR Honorary Award and Tamil Nadu State Film Award for Best Film (second prize). Endrendrum Kadhal, Nenjinile, and Minsara Kanna followed.[34]
- Kannukkul Nilavu (2000) was his 25th film; Kushi and Priyamaanavale were commercially successful romantic films.[35]
- 2001 successes: Friends, Badri, Shahjahan.
- 2002 films: Thamizhan alongside Priyanka Chopra, followed by Youth and Bagavathi.[36][37]

FILM CAREER — 2003–2011: ESTABLISHED ACTOR
- 2003: Vaseegara and Pudhiya Geethai.
- Thirumalai changed Vijay's on-screen persona from charming/comedic roles to an action hero and was a turning point.[38][39]
- Udhaya was released in 2004 after several delays.[40]
- Ghilli, directed by S. Dharani with Trisha and Prakash Raj, was a commercial success. It became the first Tamil film to gross over ₹500 million domestically and broke M. G. Ramachandran's Adimai Penn (1969) record for most people watching a movie during its first week.[41][42][43][44]
- Madhurey followed.[45]
- Thirupaachi (2005), Perarasu's blockbuster and directorial debut, was followed by Vijay's extended lawyer cameo in Sukran (2005), then commercially successful Sachein (2005) and Sivakasi (2005).[46][47][48]
- Aathi (2006) was an average grosser and a 2005 Telugu remake of Athanokkade.[49]
- Pokkiri (2007), directed by Prabhu Deva, became one of Vijay's highest-grossing films at that time.[50]
- Azhagiya Tamil Magan featured Vijay in his first dual role, as protagonist and antagonist, and was commercially successful outside India.[51][52][53]
- Kuruvi (2008) was an action comedy.[54][55]
- Villu and Vettaikaaran (2009) were average domestic successes but more successful overseas.[56][57][58] Vijay remained one of the highest-paid actors despite moderate success.[59]
- Sura (2010) was Vijay's 50th film as an actor.[60][61]
- Kaavalan (2011), directed by Siddique, received a positive response and grossed ₹102 crore worldwide. It screened at the Shanghai International Film Festival in China and was commercially successful in China.[62][63][64][65]
- Velayudham, directed by M. Raja, released during Diwali and became one of the top-grossing films of 2011.[42]

FILM CAREER — 2012–2022: COMMERCIAL SUCCESS AND STARDOM
- Nanban (2012) was a commercial success and a remake of Hindi film 3 Idiots.[66][67]
- Thuppakki, directed by A. R. Murugadoss, became the third Tamil film to gross more than ₹100 crore domestically and Vijay's most successful film at the time, grossing over ₹121 crore.[68][69]
- Thalaivaa (2013), directed by A. L. Vijay, received mixed reviews.[70][42]
- Jilla (2014) was a box office hit.[71][72]
- Kaththi (2014) featured Vijay in another dual role, received positive reviews, and collected ₹130 crore, becoming one of the highest-grossing Tamil films of 2014.[73]
- Puli (2015), directed by Chimbu Deven, received mixed to poor reviews but was among the highest-grossing films of 2015.[74][75][76][77][78]
- Theri (2016), directed by Atlee, received mixed reviews and became the second-highest-grossing Tamil film of 2016, grossing over ₹150 crore.[79][80]
- Bairavaa (2017) received mixed reviews.[81]
- Mersal (2017) was a commercial success and the first Vijay film to gross over ₹250 crore.[73][82] It won Best Foreign Language Film at the National Film Awards UK in 2018; Vijay received a best supporting actor nomination.[83] It was released in Japan and China and screened at the Bucheon International Fantastic Film Festival in South Korea.[84][85][86]
- Sarkar (2018) entered the 100 Crore Club within two days, was released in 80 countries, and became Vijay's second film to gross over ₹250 crore.[87][88][89][90]
- Bigil (2019), a football action film, received mixed reviews but Vijay was praised for his character portrayal. It earned more than ₹300 crore and was the first Tamil film released in Egypt and Jordan.[91][92][93][94]
- Master (2021), directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj, received mixed to positive reviews, collected over ₹300 crore worldwide, and was a box office success.[95][96][97][98][99]
- Beast (2022), a dark comedy action film, received mixed reviews, performed well overseas, collected more than ₹300 crore worldwide, and became one of Netflix's most viewed films globally in 2022.[100][101][102][103][104][105][106][107][108]

FILM CAREER — 2023–2026: CONTINUED SUCCESS
- Varisu (2023), directed by Vamshi Paidipally, was a commercial success, grossing over ₹300 crore.[109][110][111][112]
- Leo (2023) received mixed reviews but was commercially successful, became one of the highest-grossing Indian films of the year, and grossed more than ₹600 crore.[113][114][115][116]
- The Greatest of All Time had mixed reviews and theatrical revenue of more than ₹450 crore.[117][118][119]
- In February 2024, Vijay announced he would do only one more film, later titled Jana Nayagan.[120][121][122]
- Jana Nayagan was reportedly inspired by the Telugu film Bhagavanth Kesari.[123]
- It was scheduled for 9 January 2026, delayed due to censorship issues, and leaked on piracy websites on 9 April 2026 before official theatrical release.[124]
- Jana Nayagan (2026) opened in theatres on 23 July and had a bumper opening despite mixed reviews, with fans attending to see Vijay "one last time" on the big screen.[125][126]

MUSIC CAREER
- Vijay is considered one of the most prolific singers among Tamil actors.[127][128]
- He has sung for composers including Ilaiyaraaja and A. R. Rahman.[129][130]
- Singing debut: "Bombay City Sukkha Rotti" in Rasigan (1994).
- After recording his 25th song, "Vaadi Vaadi" in Sachein (2005), he took a sabbatical from singing to focus on acting.
- He returned with "Google Google" in Thuppakki (2012), which won Favourite Song of the Year at the Vijay Awards and earned a best playback singer nomination at the South Indian International Movie Awards.[131][132]
- In 2015, he was nominated for Best Playback Singer at the Filmfare Award South for "Yaendi Yaendi".[133]
- In 2016, he was again nominated in the same category for "Selfie Pulla".[134]

POLITICAL CAREER
- Vijay launched his fan club Vijay Makkal Iyakkam in 2009.[135][136]
- The organisation supported the AIADMK-led alliance in the 2011 Tamil Nadu assembly elections.[137]
- In February 2019, Vijay opposed the Citizenship Amendment Act 2019, saying it would disrupt the social religious harmony of the nation.[138]
- His fan-club members contested the 2022 Tamil Nadu local elections and won 115 seats.[139]
- On 2 February 2024, Vijay announced Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK).[140][141]
- On 27 September 2025, 41 people died in a crowd crush during a political rally addressed by Vijay in Karur. He condoled the deaths and announced ex-gratia payments to the next of kin of the dead and injured.[142][143]
- In October 2025, the Supreme Court transferred the case to Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI); the Tamil Nadu Government urged the Supreme Court to vacate the order.[144][145]
- Vijay was questioned by the CBI at Delhi headquarters on 12 and 19 January 2026 regarding the incident, including his delayed arrival and decision to leave early after the situation worsened.[146][147][148]
- He was questioned for a third time in March 2026 at CBI Headquarters at Delhi.[149][150]
- On 30 January 2026, Vijay said his role models include M. G. Ramachandran, J. Jayalalithaa and M. Karunanidhi, in his first NDTV interview since entering politics.[151]

CHIEF MINISTER OF TAMIL NADU (2026–PRESENT)
- Further information: C. Joseph Vijay ministry
- Vijay-led TVK debuted in the 2026 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly election and emerged as the single largest party.
- Vijay contested Perambur and Trichy East and won both. He resigned from Trichy East and retained Perambur.[152]
- He became the first Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu since Jayalalithaa in 1991 to contest and win from two constituencies, later vacating one.[153]
- On 6 May 2026, Vijay met the Governor and submitted a formal claim to form the government, asserting that TVK had the requisite majority. The Governor was not satisfied with the majority claims and did not invite Vijay to form the government on 7 May 2026. The development raised questions about legislative-support verification, gubernatorial discretion, and constitutional conventions.[154]
- On 10 May 2026, Vijay was sworn in as the ninth Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, ending six decades of Dravidian-party dominance in the state.
- The government is a coalition with Congress as a partner; VCK, CPI, CPI(M), and IUML initially extended outside support to TVK while continuing their alliance with the DMK-led SPA.[155][156]
- VCK and IUML subsequently withdrew from the DMK front and joined the Cabinet in the TVK-led coalition. The Left parties declared they were no longer part of the DMK alliance.[157]

TAX LITIGATION
- In 2015, the Income Tax Department searched actor Vijay's premises and disclosed an additional ₹15 crore in income for financial year 2015–16, including payments related to Puli.
- Vijay paid tax on the disclosed amount, but the department imposed a ₹1.5 crore penalty under Section 271AAB(1) of the Income Tax Act, stating disclosure occurred only after the search.
- Vijay challenged the penalty before the Madras High Court in 2022, arguing the proceedings were time-barred. The Income Tax Department maintained the penalty was valid.
- As of 2025, the matter remained pending, with interim protection against recovery of the penalty.[180][181][182]
- On 5 February 2020, the Income Tax Department raided Vijay's Chennai residence over potential tax evasion and investments in immovable properties.[183][184] Officials later stated that nothing significant was found.[185]
- On 13 July 2021, the Madras High Court dismissed Vijay's 2012 writ petition seeking Entry Tax exemption for his Rolls-Royce Ghost imported from the United Kingdom and imposed a ₹0.1 million (US$1,000) fine.[186]
- On 27 July 2021, a two-judge bench stayed the earlier order.[187]
- On 25 January 2022, the court dismissed the critical statements made by the earlier judge; on 15 July 2022, the court declared that no fine shall be imposed.[188][189][190]
